# ATS Resume Intelligence Engine

# Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Vision Statement

The ATS Resume Intelligence Engine aims to become a trusted platform for evaluating resumes against modern Applicant Tracking Systems through transparent, explainable, and evidence-based scoring.

Rather than relying on black-box evaluations, the platform is designed to provide deterministic results that users can understand, validate, and improve.

The long-term vision is to build an end-to-end Resume Intelligence Platform that helps candidates create stronger resumes while enabling organizations to perform more accurate and explainable resume screening.

---

# Problem Statement

Recruiters receive hundreds or thousands of resumes for a single position.

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are used to automatically screen these resumes before they reach human reviewers.

Most existing ATS scoring platforms provide a numeric score but do not clearly explain:

- Why a resume received that score.
- Which requirements were matched.
- Which skills were unsupported.
- How the score was calculated.
- Whether the detected matches were supported by actual evidence.

As a result, candidates often optimize resumes through keyword stuffing or trial and error instead of understanding genuine alignment with job requirements.

- Explainable

- Consistent

---

# Product Goals

The platform is designed to achieve the following goals.

## Explainable ATS Scoring

Every ATS score should be traceable to measurable factors.

Users should understand:

- What matched.
- What did not match.
- Why deductions occurred.
- Which evidence supports every matched requirement.

---

## Evidence-Based Matching

Requirements are considered matched only when supported by resume evidence.

Evidence may originate from

- Experience
- Projects
- Certifications
- Skills
- Professional Summary

This approach discourages superficial keyword matching.

---

## Deterministic Scoring

The same Resume and Job Description should always produce the same result.

Scoring should remain independent of randomness or non-repeatable AI outputs.

---

## Integrity Protection

The platform should identify attempts to manipulate ATS scores through techniques such as

- Keyword stuffing
- Unsupported skills
- Hidden text
- Duplicate keywords
- Artificial score inflation

---

## Reusable Architecture

The system should be modular so that individual engines can evolve independently while maintaining consistent scoring behavior.

Every capability developed for this platform should follow these principles.

- Explainability over opacity.
- Evidence over assumptions.
- Determinism over randomness.
- Quality over keyword stuffing.
- Modularity over monolithic design.
- Continuous improvement through benchmarking and calibration.

The ATS Resume Intelligence Engine is designed to move beyond traditional keyword-based resume evaluation by combining deterministic algorithms, evidence-based validation, and transparent scoring into a single, explainable ATS intelligence platform.
